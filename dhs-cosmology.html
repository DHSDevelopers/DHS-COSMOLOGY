Astronomer · Writer · Theorist

DANIYAL
DHS COSMOLOGY

"We are a way for the cosmos to know itself — and I have devoted my work to deepening that knowing."

I am an independent researcher and writer working at the frontier of modern cosmology. My work brings together rigorous mathematics, observational data, and bold speculative thinking to probe the deepest questions about our universe.

From dark energy to quantum gravity, black hole thermodynamics to the topology of spacetime — I pursue the questions that lie just beyond what current physics can comfortably answer.

DHS Cosmology is my public archive: papers, essays, and speculations shared openly with the scientific community and curious minds everywhere.
